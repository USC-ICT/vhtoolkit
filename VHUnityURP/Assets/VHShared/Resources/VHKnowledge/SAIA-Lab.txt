SAIA Lab at USC ICT - Overview
The SAIA Lab page on the ICT website is https://ict.usc.edu/research/labs-groups/saia/. Information current as of July 2026.

The Simulations, Architectures & Intelligent Agents Lab (SAIA) is a research lab at the University of Southern California Institute for Creative Technologies (USC ICT). The lab is led by Arno Hartholt. Core members are Edward Fast, Senior Architect and Tech Lead, and Andrew Leeds, Technical Support Lead.

SAIA develops interactive, autonomous, and controllable agents, the worlds they inhabit, and the architectures and tools to build both. The lab's work spans hardening research software into reusable platforms, integrating academic and industry technologies, and designing simulation-based intelligent systems for training, healthcare, education, workforce development, and entertainment.

SAIA maintains two core platforms. The first is the Virtual Human Toolkit (VHToolkit), a modular platform for creating real-time embodied conversational agents - interactive 3D characters that can listen, understand, speak, and respond with appropriate nonverbal behavior. The VHToolkit integrates audio-visual sensing, speech recognition, natural language processing with large language models, text-to-speech synthesis, lipsync, gaze, and nonverbal behavior generation. It is freely available for non-commercial use on GitHub (github.com/USC-ICT/vhtoolkit). The second platform is RIDE, the Rapid Integration & Development Environment: a real-time modeling and simulation rapid prototyping testbed framework that targets multiple simulation environments and game engines, including Unity, Unreal Engine (work in progress), Battle Road's AtomEngine (work in progress), JVT (planned), and AFSIM (planned), used for military simulation research including human-machine teaming, drones, and autonomous vehicles. RIDE is available under Government Purpose Rights. See https://ride.ict.usc.edu/ for RIDE details.

Major projects associated with the lab and its collaborations include BRAVEMIND (virtual reality exposure therapy for PTSD treatment), VITA (job interview skills training with virtual humans), Battle Buddy (a wellness mobile app for veterans), and Personalized Avatars (high-fidelity digital doubles for military training).

SAIA is the main R&D integration group of USC ICT and partners extensively with other ICT labs and groups, including Medical Virtual Reality (MedVR), the Virtual Human Therapeutics Lab (VHTL), the Mixed Reality Lab (MxR), the Dialogue group, Narrative Group, HATS Lab, Geospatial Terrain, Intelligent Human Perception Lab, the Vision and Graphics Lab, and Learning Sciences. The lab's overall aim is to move virtual human and simulation research out of the lab and onto the platforms where it can be used - by researchers, clinicians, educators, and trainers.
