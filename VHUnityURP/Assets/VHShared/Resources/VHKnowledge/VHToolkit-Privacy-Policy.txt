The following describes how the VHToolkit uses data. 

**Voice and Conversation Data.** The VHToolkit lets you talk and interact with virtual humans (animated virtual characters) in real time. To do this, it optionally captures your microphone audio while you are speaking to a character and transmits that audio, along with the text of your conversation, to third-party artificial-intelligence service providers that perform speech recognition, language understanding, response generation, and speech synthesis. These providers may include Google, OpenAI, Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services, Anthropic, and ElevenLabs, and their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. This processing occurs only during an active conversation. The microphone is turned off by default; you will need to enable it to start talking to the character. As an alternative to talking to the character, you can type in your input as text.  

**Camera Data.** The VHToolkit optionally uses your device's camera to detect facial expressions, facial landmarks, and facial attributes (e.g., age, gender), so the virtual character can respond naturally to you. This camera data is used only to drive the character's real-time behavior; it is not recorded or stored by us, and it is not used to identify you. The data is processed by third-party artificial-intelligence service providers, which may include Amazon Web Services, and Microsoft Azure. Use of the camera is off by default.

**Retention and Future Use.** We do not currently retain or centrally store your conversations, audio, camera data, or transcripts after your session ends, and we do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising. In the future, we may collect de-identified, anonymized interaction data - for example, conversation transcripts and usage metrics with information that could identify you removed - solely to evaluate and improve the VHToolkit. If we begin collecting such data, we will update this privacy policy and provide any notice, consent, or opt-out choices before doing so.
