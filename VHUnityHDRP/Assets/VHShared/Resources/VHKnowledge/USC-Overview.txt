University of Southern California (USC) - Overview
The USC website is https://www.usc.edu. Information current as of July 2026.

The University of Southern California (USC) is a private research university in Los Angeles, California. Founded in 1880, it is one of the oldest private research universities in the western United States. USC describes itself as a great private research university that serves the public good across Los Angeles and Southern California.

USC operates two main campuses in Los Angeles: the University Park Campus, the primary campus near downtown Los Angeles, and the Health Sciences Campus. The university comprises 23 schools and academic divisions, including the Dornsife College of Letters, Arts and Sciences; the Viterbi School of Engineering; the Marshall School of Business; the Keck School of Medicine; the Gould School of Law; the School of Cinematic Arts; the Annenberg School for Communication and Journalism; the Rossier School of Education; the School of Architecture; the Mann School of Pharmacy; the Thornton School of Music; the Kaufman School of Dance; and the School of Dramatic Arts, among others.

USC is a major research university, with schools, centers, and institutes conducting research in areas including medicine and disease, technology and artificial intelligence, and environmental solutions. The USC Viterbi School of Engineering is home to several prominent research institutes, including the Institute for Creative Technologies (ICT) and the Information Sciences Institute (ISI).

USC's athletics teams are the Trojans, whose traditions include the Trojan Marching Band, the mascot Traveler, and the fight song "Fight On." USC has a large and active international community and one of the largest alumni networks of any university, often referred to as the Trojan Family.

The USC School of Cinematic Arts is the oldest and among the most prominent film schools in the world, reflecting the university's deep ties to the entertainment industry - ties that also shaped the founding of the Institute for Creative Technologies, which combines simulation research with storytelling and production techniques from Hollywood.
